02About

I study how ideas from biology can help us build AI systems that are more efficient, adaptive, and aligned with the way intelligence works in the real world.

My work sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, neuroscience, and machine learning. I’m especially interested in models that can process information over time without spending more energy than they need to.

Before joining PolyU, I studied applied mathematics at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China and Xi’an Jiaotong University.
