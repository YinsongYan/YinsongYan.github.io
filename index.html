My name is Yinsong Yan (闫银松). I am currently a PhD student in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Applied Mathematics from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2020, and my master’s degree in Applied Mathematics from Xi’an Jiaotong University in 2023.

My research focuses on developing brain-inspired, energy-efficient algorithms by bridging biological neural mechanisms with computational efficiency. I am particularly interested in spiking neural networks, neuromorphic computing, and brain-inspired machine intelligence.

My work aims to draw insights from the brain’s energy-efficient information processing to improve algorithmic performance for complex tasks while reducing computational overhead.

I am always open to research discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to reach out by email.
